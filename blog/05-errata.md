Hey everyone! This is a tiny update – I've squashed some bugs that slipped through the cracks for 0.5. You should now be able to build warbands and get games in without hitting anything gamebreaking.

## Core RUles
- I had to pull an emergency stop and revert the **climbing rules**, as the new rules were half-baked and RAW led to fighters being able to climb 12” in one Move action (by interacting with ladders and hooks). We’ll keep collecting feedback on the original climbing rules throughout the playtest and will explore more options.
- Several copy tweaks, removal of PDF page references, clarification of rules and other quality of life fixes

## Faction Rules
_**Possessed**_
- Mutants was missing the MUTANT keyword ![🫣](https://discord.com/assets/7b771c26248de782.svg) 

_**Sisters of Sigmar**_
- Removed Bows from Sisters of Sigmar (added by mistake)

_**Clan Eshin**_
- Weeping Blades now come as a pair and has the Two-Handed weapon rule.
- Assassin Adept ability `One With The Shadows` was listed as a [Trait] and didn’t render on the wiki, now properly shown as a [Triple]
- Rephrasing of faction ability `Hidden Advance` to trigger in combat phase rather than battle round (combat phase was always the intent!)

**That's all! Thank you for being awesome and keep dropping them hobby projects in ⁠minis and post your battle reports in ⁠battle-reports!**
