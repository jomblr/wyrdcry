# Beastmen Raiders
**The Beastmen are brutish, mutated creatures of Chaos who haunt the deep forests of the Old World. They build no cities and have no society, for order is unnatural and hateful to them. Roaming in small warbands led by the strongest and most ferocious of their kind, they strike without warning, burning farms, butchering cattle and tearing down every structure of made by mankind. They now gather in great numbers in the forests around Mordheim, eager to gather the "shards of the howling star" to add to their herdstones.**
