# Elf Rangers
**Elven Rangers, or ‘The Dispossessed’ as they are known by their Asur kin, are the descendants of the High Elf colonies that were ravaged and abandoned after the War of the Beard. Bitter and proud, they roam the wilds of the Old World seeking lost treasures and glories of their ancient households. Now, they are drawn to the east, to the corrupted city of Mordheim, where something foul and evil calls for their direct intervention.**
