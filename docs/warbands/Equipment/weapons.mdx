# Weapons
Weapons are used when making attack actions and come in two variants: **melee weapons** and **ranged weapons**. A fighter may be equipped with up to two melee weapons and one ranged weapon.

### Dual Wielding
A fighter that is equipped with two melee weapons are considered to be **dual wielding**, and may use both weapons when making melee attack actions.

### Unarmed fighters
A fighter that isn't equipped with any melee weapon is considered to be unarmed, and uses the Unarmed weapon profile.

### Natural Weapons {#natural-weapons}
Some fighters (such as `beasts`) cannot be equipped with weapons, and instead have natural weapons listed directly on their fighter profile, representing claws, jaws, and other physical features.
