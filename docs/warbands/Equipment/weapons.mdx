# Weapons

Weapons are used when making attack actions and come in two variants: **melee weapons** and **ranged weapons**. A fighter may be equipped with up to two melee weapons and one ranged weapon.

### Dual Wielding

If a fighter is equipped with two melee weapons of the same type (for example, two swords), they are considered to be **dual wielding**. While a fighter is dual wielding, improve the Attack characteristic of the weapon they are using by 1.

### Natural Weapons {#natural-weapons}

Some fighters (such as `beasts`) have natural weapons listed directly on their fighter profile, representing claws, jaws, and other physical features. Natural weapons function like regular weapons, but are a part of the fighter's body and can never be replaced, sold or discarded.
