# Starting a warband
## Step 1: Get a warband roster
---
You may either [download a roster](/files/warband-roster.pdf) or use the [Warband Builder](/warband-builder).

## Step 2: Select a faction
---
All factions have a faction entry that contains all of the special rules, fighter profiles and weapons lists unique to that faction. Select one of them and note down the faction name on your warband roster.

- [Clan Eshin](core-factions/clan-eshin.mdx)
- [Mercenaries](core-factions/mercenaries.mdx)
- [Witch Hunters](core-factions/witch-hunters.mdx)
- [The Cult of The Possessed](core-factions/possessed.mdx)
- [Sisters of Sigmar](core-factions/sisters-of-sigmar.mdx)
- [The Undead](core-factions/undead.mdx)

:::info[]
### Playtest Factions
If your gaming group agrees, you may decide to also allow factions that are currently being playtested for the game. Note that these factions are in active development and may change over the coming months.

- [Beastmen Raiders](playtest-factions/beastmen-raiders.mdx)
- [Elf Rangers](playtest-factions/elf-rangers.mdx)

## Step 3: Recruit fighters
---
Begin recruiting fighters from your faction by paying their recruitment fee, while following these restrictions:
- The total cost of your warband cannot exceed 1000 gold coins.
- Your warband must include at least 3 fighters, and no more fighters than is listed under ‘Warband Size’ in your faction entry.
- Your warband must include one `Leader`. No more, and no less.
- If a fighter has a number range listed next to its name, it indicates how many fighters of that type may be included in your warband. For example, a fighter with a range of 0–2 means you may only include up to two fighters of that type in your warband.

## Step 4: Equipment
---
Purchase equipment for your fighters while following these restrictions:
- You may only purchase weapons and armour listed in the fighter's faction entry, as well as common items from the [trading post](../campaigns/the-trading-post.md).
- A fighter may be equipped with up to two melee weapons, one ranged weapon and a suit of armour.
	- A fighter can forfeit one melee weapon to wear a shield.
- Any equipment marked with `Hero` may only be equipped by a `Hero`.
- `BEASTS` and `THRALLS` cannot use any equipment other than the weapons listed in their fighter profile.

## Step 5: Finishing Touches
---
### The Warband Stash
Any gold coins not spent on fighters and equipment are stored in the **warband stash** and may be used to recruit new fighters and buy new equipment during a campaign.

### Renown
Your `Leader` starts the campaign with 1 point of renown and may immediately select a heroic talent [(see Renown)](../campaigns/the-aftermath-sequence/earn-renown.md). This represents the fighter's experience and notoriety prior to coming to the cursed city.

### Favour & Standing
Each warband has a faction standing that represents the relationship between the warband and their faction. The process of earning favour and rising in standing is explained in detail in the aftermath sequence. For now, set your warband’s favour to 0 and their standing to **'Disposable'.**

### Calculate Reputation
Reputation is an indication of how powerful and notorious a warband is, and is primarily used for bragging rights and underdog bonuses. Reputation equals your warband’s current favour score plus the total renown of all fighters in the warband.

When you start your warband, Reputation is simply the total sum of renown from all fighters in the warband.
