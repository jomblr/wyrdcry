## Faction rules
---
All `Sisters of Sigmar` fighters have access to the following rules:

### [Trait] Sigmar’s Blessing
This fighter may call for one of Sigmar's Blessings by spending saved Wyrd Dice:

- **Hearts of Steel:** When this fighter is asked to take a Bravery test, you may discard one saved Wyrd Dice to automatically pass the test.
- **Righteous Fury:** During this fighter’s activation, after making a hit roll, you may discard one saved Wyrd Dice to turn a hit into a critical hit.
- **Sigmar Protects:** During an enemy fighter’s activation, you may discard one saved Wyrd Dice to perform a reaction with this fighter without needing or spending an action to do so.

### [Reaction] Martyrdom
A fighter can make this reaction when they suffer enough damage to take them out of action, but before removing them from the battlefield. Add 1 Wyrd Dice to your saved Wyrd Dice. If this fighter has the `HERO` keyword, add 2 instead. If this fighter has the `LEADER keyword`, add 3 instead.
