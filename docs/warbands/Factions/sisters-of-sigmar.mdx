# Sisters of Sigmar

<FactionReference factionId="sisters-of-sigmar" />

## Warband size
---

A Sisters of Sigmar warband may consist of up to 15 fighters

## Faction rules
---
:::inverse
### [Faction Trait] Sigmar’s Blessing
All `Sisters of Sigmar` fighters may call for one of Sigmar's Blessings by spending saved Wyrd Dice:

- **Hearts of Steel:** When a fighter is asked to take a Bravery test, you may discard one saved Wyrd Dice to automatically pass the test.
- **Righteous Fury:** During a fighter’s activation, after making a hit roll, you may discard one saved Wyrd Dice to turn a hit into a critical hit.
- **Sigmar Protects:** During an enemy fighter’s activation, you may discard one saved Wyrd Dice to perform a reaction with a fighter without needing or spending an action to do so.
:::

In addition, all `Sisters of Sigmar` fighters have the following trait:

### [Trait] Fervent Prayer
Whenever this fighter makes the Wait action they may pray to Sigmar by taking a Bravery test. If it passes, add 1 Wyrd Dice to your saved Wyrd Dice.
