# Cult of the Possessed

<FactionReference factionId="possessed" />

## Warband size
---

A Cult of the Possessed warband may consist of up to 15 fighters

## Faction rules
---

:::inverse
### [Faction Trait] Blood Offerings

Whenever a `Possessed` fighter takes an enemy fighter out of action with a Melee Attack action, they are rewarded by the Shadowlord and may claim one of his dark blessings:

- **Spasmatic Surge:** The fighter makes a free Move action up to 3”.
- **Dark Invigoration:** Remove d3 damage points from the fighter.
- **Temporary Possession:** For the fighter’s next Attack action, you may increase its Attacks characteristic by 1.
:::

In addition, all `POSSESSED` fighters have the following reaction:

### [Reaction] Glorious Death
A fighter can make this reaction when they suffer enough damage to take them out of action, but before removing them from the battlefield. Add 1 Wyrd Dice to your saved Wyrd Dice.

## Mutants and Mutations
---
Whenever a `MUTANT` gains renown, before making a characteristics increase or selecting a talent, it must make a **Stability test**. Roll a dice, and add 1 to the roll for each mutation the fighter has, then consult the table below. A roll of 1 is always treated as a 1, no matter the amount of mutations the fighter has.

### Stability test

|Dice Result|Effect|
|:-:|:-|
|1-3|**The flesh slumbers:** Make a characteristic increase for this fighter (see advancements) or select a talent (if the fighter is a `HERO`).|
|4-8|**New Mutation:** Roll 2d6 and consult the mutation table below. If the result is a mutation this fighter already has, roll again.|
|9+|**Consumed by Chaos:** The fighter devolves into a thrashing mass of eyes and tentacles and disappears into the ruins. Remove this fighter from your warband roster, and gain 1 point of Favour.|

### Mutation Table

|D66 Roll|Mutations|
|:-:|:-|
|11-13|**Vessel of the Gods:** When this fighter is deployed to the battlefield, you may add an additional wyrd dice to your saved wyrd dice.|
|14-16|**Deformed Wings:** Once per battle round, when this fighter makes a move action, it may count as flying; however, when flying, they cannot move vertically upwards more than 3". In addition, this fighter does not suffer damage when falling, regardless of the distance.|
|21-23|**Tentacles:** Can be used as a melee weapon when making attack actions, using the following weapon profile; **Range 2” \| 3 Attacks \| 2/3 Damage \| Whiplash**.|
|24-26|**Black Blood:** Allocate 1 damage to every visible fighter within 1” of this fighter, whenever this fighter is allocated damage from a critical hit.|
|31-33|**Temporal Instability:** Once per battle round, when this fighter makes a Move action, it may reduce its move characteristic by 3 to treat an obstacle as insignificant terrain.|
|34-36|**Horrific Appearance:** This fighter gains the `Terrifying` keyword.|
|41-43|**Cloud of flies:** Subtract 1 from the Fight and Shoot characteristics (to a minimum of 1) of all fighters within 2” of this fighter.|
|44-46|**Great Claw:** Can be used as a melee weapon when making attack actions, using the following weapon profile; **Range 1” \| 2 Attacks \| 3/4 Damage \| Pierce 3**.|
|51-53|**Metabolic overdrive:** Once per battle round, this fighter can make a free move action. If it does, allocate D3 damage points to this fighter.|
|54-56|**Rapid Regeneration:** Each time this fighter is activated, you can remove D3 damage points allocated to them.|
|61-63|**Daemonic Chitin:** Whenever this fighter is the target by an attack action, you may subtract 1 from the damage points allocated by each hit and critical hit (to a minimum of 1).|
|64-66|**Scorpion Tail:** Can be used as an **off-hand** weapon when making melee attack actions, using the following weapon profile; **Range 1” \| 1 Attack \| 3/6 Damage \| Pierce 1**.|
