# Witch Hunters

## Warband size
---
A Witch Hunter warband may consist of up to 15 fighters

## Faction rules
---

:::inverse
### [Faction Trait] Religious Zeal
As battle is joined, Witch Hunter warbands are consumed by religious fervour. This is represented in-game by Zeal. Zeal is increased through the use of certain faction abilities, and as it reaches new thresholds, you must apply the corresponding effect to all friendly fighters (excluding `HIRED SWORDS`). The effects are cumulative and last for the entire battle.

#### Condemned fighters
Some effects require an enemy fighter to be **condemned**, which is applied through the use of the ‘Condemn’ ability and the ‘Sudden Accusation’ reaction. Use a condemned token to keep track of condemned fighters.

|  Zeal |  Effect |
|:---:|:---|
|0+|**Grim:** When a friendly fighter makes an attack action against a condemned enemy fighter, increase their Fight or Shoot characteristic by 1.|
|5+|**Wrathful:** When a friendly fighter makes an attack action against a condemned enemy fighter, you may turn 1 hit into a critical hit.|
|10+|**Frenzied:** Increase the Armour characteristic of all friendly fighters by 1.|
:::

In addition, all `Witch Hunter` fighters without the `Beast` keyword have the following reaction:

### [Reaction] Sudden Accusation
A fighter can make this reaction when a visible friendly fighter is taken out of action by a visible enemy fighter. That enemy fighter is condemned. Then, increase Zeal by 1.
