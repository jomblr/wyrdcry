# Clan Eshin

It is a testament to the black-clad agents of Clan Eshin that none but rambling madmen believe the world of men is watched, keenly and closely, by a race of tunnel-dwelling ratmen.

For centuries they have infiltrated the cities of the Empire, gathering secrets for their dark masters and slitting the throats of any who would expose their kin. It comes as no surprise, then, that it was Eshin who first learned of the calamity that befell Mordheim, and of the stones of power uncovered there.

In his insidious wisdom, the Nightmaster has declared a war of misdirection upon skavendom. While the Grey Seers are engrossed in quelling clan rivalries and chasing carefully planted rumours, the triads descend upon the cursed city, eager to prove themselves and to elevate the status of their clan.
