<FactionReference factionId="clan-eshin" />

## Warband size
---
A Clan Eshin warband may consist of up to 20 fighters

## Faction rules
---
:::inverse
### [Faction Trait] Black-clad Killers
When setting up a battle, you may decide to split your warband into four search parties instead of three. The fighters in the fourth search party are **hidden** and kept in reserve – even if the deployment map doesn't feature reserve search parties.

In the second or third battle round, when it is your turn to activate a fighter, you may **reveal** and activate a hidden fighter instead. Set up the fighter on the battlefield floor more than 6" from enemy fighters and more than 3" from objective markers and wyrdstone tokens. During that fighter's activation, enemy fighters cannot make reactions.
:::

In addition, all `CLAN ESHIN` fighters have the following special rules:

### [Trait] Strength in Numbers
When this fighter makes an attack action, if the target is flanked, this fighter’s weapons gain the <WeaponRule id="pierce-1" /> weapon rule.

### [Double] Slink Away
A fighter can only use this ability if they are within 1" of an enemy fighter. This fighter makes a free Retreat action.

## Unique Equipment

### Smoke Bomb (15gc)
The bearer may use this item to make a free Retreat action. During that action, enemy fighters within 3” of the bearer cannot make reactions.
