## Faction rules
---
All `CLAN ESHIN` fighters have access to the following rules:

### Hidden Advance

When deploying a `CLAN ESHIN` warband to the battlefield, you may keep one search party in reserve. If you do, the fighters in that search party are **hidden**. In the beginning of each battleround after the first, before any fighters are activated, you may set up any amount of hidden fighters on the battlefield, at least 12” from your opponents battlefield edge, and further than 6” away from enemy fighters and objective markers.

### [Trait] Shadowy Killers
When this fighter is activated, if it is not visible to enemy fighters, you may increase their Fight or Shoot characteristic by 1 in their next attack action.

### [Double] Slink Away
This fighter makes a free Retreat action.
