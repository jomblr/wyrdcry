# Getting Started
To get started, I recommend everyone to go through the [general principles](./general-principles.md) of the game and the rules for [how to play a battle](./starting-a-battle.md).

This will get you up to speed with the basics, and is a good starting point for when you [start your warband](../warbands/starting-warband.md).
