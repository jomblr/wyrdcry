# Channel Magic
---
A `Wizard` can channel magic to generate or upgrade ability dice to be used for magical abilities. When making the Channel Magic action, you may add a new **[Double]** with a value of 3 to your ability dice, or choose one existing ability dice and apply one of the following effects to it:

- Upgrade a **[Double]** to a **[Triple]**, or a **[Triple]** to a **[Quad]**.
- Increase the value of an ability dice by 3 (to a maximum of 6).

Then, you must roll 2D6 and consult the [Magical Calamity](#magical-calamity) table below. The channeled ability dice must be used for a *magical ability* during the `Wizard`’s activation; they cannot be saved for another fighter and are discarded if unused.

A `Wizard` can channel magic multiple times for the same ability dice (e.g., a **[Double]** can first be upgraded to a **[Triple]**, and then have its value increased).

### Magical Calamity
|   2D6    | Effect                                                                                                                                  |
| :------: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  **2**   | **Calamitous Detonation:** Allocate 5 damage points to each fighter within 3” of the `Wizard`. Then, the `Wizard` is taken out of action. |
| **3-4**  | **Miscast:** Allocate 5 damage points to the `Wizard`. The Channel Magic action ends and no ability dice are added or upgraded.               |
| **5-9**  | **Barely Contained Power:** Allocate 3 damage points to the `Wizard`.                                                                   |
| **10-12** | **Controlled Channeling:** The `Wizard` suffers no ill effects.                                                                        |
