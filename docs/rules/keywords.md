# Keywords

Keywords describe distinctive features of a fighter: the race they belong to, the
faction that recruited them, and the archetype they follow. When referenced in a
rule, keywords are always written in `UPPERCASE`. A rule that names a keyword in
the plural (for example, `HEROES`) refers to the singular keyword (`HERO`).

A fighter carries one racial keyword, one faction keyword, and any number of
fighter keywords. Weapons carry no keywords: what a weapon brings to an attack
action is a **special rule**, listed in the
[special rules glossary](/docs/warbands/Equipment/weapons#special-rules).

## Racial Keywords

A fighter's racial keyword sets how far each characteristic can be raised when
they make a [Characteristics Increase](/docs/campaigns/renown#characteristic-increase),
and some rules single a race out: a weapon with the Blessed special rule strikes
harder against the undead, and the Witch Hunters reserve their zeal for `DAEMON`
and `WIZARD`.

- `BEASTMAN`
- `DAEMON`
- `DWARF`
- `ELF`
- `GHOUL`
- `HUMAN`
- `MONSTER`
- `OGRE`
- `SKAVEN`
- `VAMPIRE`

`BEAST` and `THRALL` are carried in the same place on a fighter's profile, but
they bring rules of their own and are listed under Fighter Keywords below.

## Faction Keywords

Every fighter carries the keyword of the warband that recruited them. Faction
rules name it to say who they apply to.

- `CLAN ESHIN`
- `HIRED SWORD`
- `MERCENARIES`
- `POSSESSED`
- `SISTERS OF SIGMAR`
- `UNDEAD`
- `WITCH HUNTERS`

## Fighter Keywords

- <span Id='Leader'>`LEADER`</span>
	- Friendly fighters within 6" can use the `LEADER`'s Bravery characteristic when taking [Panic tests](/docs/rules/the-combat-phase/panic)
	- If killed, a [new leader must be selected](/docs/campaigns/aftermath-sequence#death-of-a-leader)
- <span Id='Hero'>`HERO`</span>
	- Can use equipment marked with <HeroOnly />
	- Can make a [Heroic Reaction](/docs/rules/the-combat-phase/reactions#heroic-reaction) once per battle
	- Can make a characteristics increase or select a trait when [gaining renown](/docs/campaigns/renown)
	- Can be sent to find items at the [Trading Post](/docs/campaigns/the-trading-post)
	- Must roll on the [Hero Injury Table](/docs/campaigns/aftermath-sequence#hero-injuries) when taken out of action
- <span Id='Wizard'>`WIZARD`</span>
	- Can use magical abilities
	- Can make the [Channel Magic](/docs/rules/the-combat-phase/actions/channel-magic) action
	- Cannot be equipped with armour
- <span Id='Henchman'>`HENCHMAN`</span>
	- Can make a characteristics increase when [gaining renown](/docs/campaigns/renown)
	- Becomes a `HERO` when reaching 4 renown
	- Must roll on the [Henchman Injury Table](/docs/campaigns/aftermath-sequence#henchman-injuries) when taken out of action
- <span Id='Beast'>`BEAST`</span>
	- Never gain experience or renown
	- Cannot make scenario actions
	- Cannot be given equipment, but uses [natural weapons](/docs/warbands/Equipment/weapons#natural-weapons)
- <span Id='Thrall'>`THRALL`</span>
	- Bound to the `VAMPIRE` leading the warband
	- Cannot be equipped with any weapons, armour or equipment
	- Never earn experience or gain renown
	- Cannot carry treasure tokens
	- Automatically pass any Bravery test they are required to take
- <span Id='Mutant'>`MUTANT`</span>
	- Must make a [Stability test](/docs/warbands/Factions/possessed#mutants-and-mutations) when gaining renown, before making a characteristics increase or selecting a talent
- <span Id='Terrifying'>`TERRIFYING`</span>
	- A fighter making an attack action against a `TERRIFYING` enemy must take a Bravery test or become Panicked. On a passed test, that fighter ignores further Bravery tests caused by `TERRIFYING` enemies for the remainder of their activation. See [Panic](/docs/rules/the-combat-phase/panic#terrifying)
