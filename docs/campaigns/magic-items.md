# Magic Items
---
During a campaign, your warband may obtain **magical items** that your fighters can bear in battle, typically by [**exploring districts**](the-aftermath-sequence/exploration.md) or by stealing them from enemy fighters.

Each magical item is unique — only one of each exists in the entire campaign. If you roll to determine which magical item you've found and the result is an item in another warband's possession, re-roll until you obtain one that isn't.

When you obtain a magical item, you may select a fighter to equip it or store it in your warband stash. A fighter can only ever be equipped with one magical item, and should they be taken out of action, you must roll a d6 at the end of the battle. On a 4+, it has been stolen by the opposing warband.

Finally, magical items are invaluable, and bringing them to the black market would likely cause a riot. They cannot be sold, and for each magical item in your warband’s possession, you must increase your reputation by 5.

## Magical Items
---

:::encounter
### 1: The Skitterflute
*This bone pipe reeks of the sewers, yet its notes carry a soundless tune that the vermin cannot resist.*

Once per battle, the bearer may use the Gnawdoom magical ability without spending the ability dice to do so. Treat the ability value as 6.
:::

:::encounter
### 2: The Sceptre of Count Steinhardt

*This gilded and grotesque sceptre enforced count Steinhardt’s rule long before anyone dared to ask what he worshipped in the cellars beneath his palace.*

Counts as a Master-Wrought Club. If a melee attack action made by the bearer scores any critical hits, roll a dice. On a 4+, allocate 4 additional damage points to the target.
:::

:::encounter
### 3: The Phylactery of Cassandora

*Wearers struck down vanish in a flash of white light, only to reappear unharmed moments later.*

Cannot be equipped by `Vampires` or `Daemons`. Once per battle, when the bearer is the target of an attack action, when the totalled damage is enough to take the bearer out of action, roll a dice. On a 3+, the bearer is not taken out of action, and any damage points that remain to be allocated to the bearer are discarded.
:::

:::encounter
### 4: Von Adelbrecht’s Compass

*A cracked shard of wyrdstone suspended in a rusted frame. It seems to guide the bearer toward concentrations of lingering spirits.*

When the bearer is selected to explore a district, you may roll one additional exploration dice.
:::

:::encounter
### 5: The Rope of Pieter

*Twisted from shreds of old state banners, this rope stirs as though it has a will of its own, lengthening and coiling tight again at a whispered word.*

Counts as a Rope and Hook. The bearer counts as flying in the first move action they make each battle round.
:::

:::encounter
### 6: Grimoire of the Howling Choir

*This grimoire is bound in scorched skin, and holds no spells but the sound of that fateful night when the comet struck. Open it, and the city screams again.*

The bearer can open or close the grimoire as a free action. While opened, no fighter within 6” of the bearer (including the bearer) can use reactions or abilities.
:::
