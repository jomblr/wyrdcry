# Hired Swords
Hired Swords can be recruited by players when setting up a battle by paying their recruitment fee (see page XX). Each Hired Sword's fighter profile lists which warbands may recruit them. Hired Swords are not added to the warband roster, do not count toward a warband's size limit, and leave the warband at the end of the battle.

During the battle, a Hired Sword considers all fighters in the warband that recruited them as friendly fighters, and all fighters in opposing warbands as enemy fighters. A Hired Sword that is taken out of action does not count when determining whether their warband is wavering.
