# Hired Swords
Hired Swords are travelling adventurers who wander between the settlements surrounding Mordheim to sell their services to the highest bidder.

## Recruiting Hired Swords

Hired Swords may be recruited in step 3 of setting up a battle, after the underdog has been determined, but before the warbands have been divided into search parties. Starting with the warband with the highest Reputation, each warband may recruit Hired Swords by paying their listed recruitment fee.

The Hired Swords are not added to the warband roster and do not count towards a warband's size limit, and at the end of the battle, they leave the warband.

### Faction Restrictions
Many Hired Swords are picky about their employer and have a list of factions that they will fight for listed in their fighter profile.

## During the battle
A Hired Sword considers all fighters in the warband that recruited them to be friendly fighters, and all other fighters on the battlefield to be enemy fighters. Hired Swords do not count when determining whether a warband is wavering and are simply removed from the battlefield when taken out of action
