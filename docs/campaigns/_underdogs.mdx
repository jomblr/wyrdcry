# Underdogs 
Over the course of a campaign, some warbands will inevitably grow stronger than others. Wether through luck or skill they will have acquired better equipment and more fighters, and while there’s always a chance that a weaker warband might prevail against such a foe, the wisest choice might often be to find easier prey. 

But the factions demand results, and will utilise their entire network of saboteurs, informants, indebted sellswords and smugglers to tip the scales and ensure a steady stream of wyrdstone out of the city of the damned. 

## Determining the Underdog 
The **underdog** is determined in step 6 of starting a battle (page 14 of the main rulebook), by comparing the Reputation score of both warbands. If one warband's Reputation score is 10 less than the other's, it is the underdog. 

### Underdog Bonus 
For each 10-point difference in reputation, the underdog gets to select an underdog bonus from the list below: 
- **Informants:** Start the battle with 2 additional Wyrd Dice. 
- **Calling in a debt:** You gain 50 gold coin to immediately spend on **HIRED SWORDS**. Any unspent gold coin are discarded. 
- **Sabotage:** Select one fighter in the enemy warband. That fighter misses the battle. 

In addition, in the aftermath sequence, each fighter in the Underdog warband earns one additional point of experience.
