# Strength Talents
---
### [Trait] Fearsome
This fighter gains the `Terrifying` keyword.

### [Trait] Oppressor
Once per battle round, when an enemy fighter makes the 'Retreat' action, this fighter can make the 'Cut Them Down' reaction without having to forfeit an action to do so.

### [Trait] Weapon Master
Select a melee weapon type. When making attack actions with weapons of that type, increase the critical damage characteristic by 1.

### [Double] Staggering Blows
A fighter can use this ability after allocating damage points to an enemy fighter with a melee attack action. That enemy fighter cannot use reactions for the rest of the battle round. In addition, if the melee attack action allocated damage points from a critical hit, the target cannot use abilities for the rest of the battle round.

### [Double] Shield Bash
This ability requires the fighter being equipped with a shield. Select an enemy fighter within 1" of this fighter. That fighter makes a free retreat action directly away from this fighter. If it cannot move away from this fighter, allocate (X/2) damage points to that fighter instead.

### [Triple] Maintain Momentum
A fighter can only use this ability if an enemy fighter has been taken down by an attack action made by them this activation. This fighter makes a bonus move action or a bonus attack action
