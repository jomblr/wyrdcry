# Strength Talents
---
### [Trait] Fearsome
This fighter gains the `Terrifying` keyword.

### [Trait] Drillmaster
When earning renown in the aftermath sequence, if this fighter wasn’t taken out of action in the battle, you may select an additional `Henchman` to be marked for greatness.

### [Trait] Oppressor
Once per battle round, when an enemy fighter makes the Retreat action, this fighter can make the ’Cut Them Down’ reaction without having to forfeit an action to do so.

### [Trait] Staggering Blows
Whenever this fighter scores a critical hit with a melee attack action, the target cannot use reactions or abilities for the rest of the battle round.

### [Trait] Weapon Master
Select a melee weapon type. When making attack actions with weapons of that type, increase its critical damage characteristic by 1.

### [Trait] Shield Bash
This fighter can use a shield to make off-hand attacks. If the off-hand attack scores a hit or critical hit, the target makes a free Retreat action directly away from this fighter.

### [Trait] Maintain Momentum
When this fighter uses the Onslaught ability, it affects each melee attack action they make until the end of their activation, instead of only their next one.

### [Quad] Executioner's Strike
*Legendary ability.* This fighter makes a free attack action. Increase the Hit Damage and Critical Damage of that attack action by (X/2).
