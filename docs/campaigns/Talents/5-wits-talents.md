# Wits Talents
---
### [Trait] Bootlicker
When collecting income in the aftermath sequence, if this fighter was not taken out of action, your warband earns 1 additional favour.

### [Trait] Charismatic
Once per battle, this fighter can use the ‘Inspiring Presence’ ability without needing or spending ability dice to do so.

### [Trait] Master Tactician
`Leader` only. When seizing the initiative in the initiative phase, if there is a tie, do not roll off. Instead the player controlling this fighter chooses which tied player has the initiative. If multiple warbands have this talent, the players of those warbands roll off, and the winner chooses which tied player has the initiative.

### [Trait] Well-Connected
Once per battle when recruiting a `Hired Sword`, roll a dice. On a 4+, you may reduce the hiring fee by 50% (rounding up).

### [Reaction] Fall Back!
A fighter can make this reaction after they have been allocated the total damage points by a melee attack action. This fighter makes a free Retreat action.

### [Triple] Warcry
Select each friendly fighter within 6” of this fighter. Those fighters are no longer panicked, and does not have to take Bravery tests until the end of the battleround.

### [Quad] Coordinated Assault
*Legendary ability.* Select 3 friendly `Henchmen` within 6” of this fighter. Each selected fighter makes either a free move action, a free retreat action, or a free attack action.
