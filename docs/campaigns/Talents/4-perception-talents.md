# Perception Talents
---
### [Trait] Eagle Eyes
When making an attack action using a bow, crossbow or handgun, this fighter may ignore the maximum range of the weapon, but if they do, they must subtract 1 from their Shoot characteristic.

### [Trait] Reckless
This fighter may target enemy fighters within 1” of friendly fighters with ranged attack actions. If they do, after making the hit rolls, roll a dice. On a 1, allocate the damage to one of the friendly fighters instead of the target.

### [Trait] Rapid Reload
At the end of each battle round, you may reload one of this fighter's weapons with the Reload special rule.

### [Trait] Scout
After making an exploration roll in the aftermath sequence, if the bearer is in the search party, you may resolve two encounters instead of one, even if one of the encounters is a mishap.

### [Trait] Surgical Precision
This fighter's weapons gain the **Piercing 1** weapon rule.

### [Trait] Trick Shot
When this fighter makes the ranged attack action, the target does not count as being in cover.

### [Reaction] Patient Hunter
Once per battle round, when an enemy fighter ends a move action visible to this fighter, this fighter can target that enemy fighter with a free ranged attack action. For that attack action, subtract 1 from this fighter's Shoot characteristic.

### [Quad] Killing Shot
*Legendary ability.* For this fighter's next ranged attack action, add (X/2) to its Shoot, Hit Damage and Critical Damage characteristics.
