# Toughness Talents
---
### [Trait] Second Wind
Each time this fighter is activated, if it is wounded, you can remove D3 damage points allocated to them.

### [Trait] Nine Lives
When rolling for permanent injuries, this fighter can re-roll the first result of 11-12. The second result stands.

### [Trait] Tough as Nails
Increase this fighter's Health characteristic by 4.

### [Trait] Bulwark
When equipped with a shield, friendly fighters within 1" of this fighter are considered to be in cover.

### [Reaction] Endure
A fighter can make this reaction after they are targeted by an attack action but before the hit rolls are made. Subtract 1 from the damage points allocated by each hit and critical hit (to a minimum of 1) from that attack action.

### [Double] Bodyguard
Select a friendly `Hero` within 1” of this fighter. Until the end of the battle round, while that `Hero` remains within 1" of this fighter, they cannot be targeted by attack actions.

### [Quad] Indomitable
*Legendary ability.* A fighter can only use this ability if an enemy fighter has been taken down by an attack action made by them this activation. Remove up to 10 damage points allocated to this fighter.
