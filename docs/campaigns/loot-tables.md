|10|**A Wyrdstone Shard**, ice cold to the touch.|
|11|**Noble Signet Ring.** Can be handed over to your faction for 1 Favour or sold for 20gc.|
|12|Rare find. Roll once on the [Greater Loot Table.](./loot-tables#greater-loot-table)|
