# Contracts
Each faction pursues its own hidden agenda, and sometimes command their warbands to perform secret errands and perilous missions while gathering wyrdstone in the city. These errands are represented in the game as **contracts**. 

## Determining Contracts 
Contracts offer warbands an additional way to earn favor from their faction apart from winning battles. Two contracts are randomly determined for each player when starting battle. To do so, roll a dice and consult the list below, then repeat this process to determine a second contract. 

Each player must select one of the contracts offered to them, starting with the defender. A player may refuse both contracts, but will lose 1 point of favor for doing so. 

### Reward 
In the aftermath sequence, if a warband successfully completed their contract, they earn 2 additional favor. If a warband failed to complete their contract, they instead lose 2 favor. 

## 1: Mark Territory 
For this contract, your opponent must select 3 terrain pieces further than 9” from their battlefield edge. During the battle, friendly fighters within 1” of such a terrain piece may **mark territory** as a scenario action. If your fighters successfuly mark all of the selected terrain pieces before the end of the battle, the contract is completed.

## 2: Assassination 
To complete this contract, your fighters must take an enemy `LEADER` out of action before the end of the battle. 

## 3: Headhunters 
At the end of the battle, compare the number of `HEROES` taken out of action by each warband. If your warband took more enemy `HEROES` out of action than your opponent’s warband did, you have completed the contract. 

## 4: Recover Item 
For this contract, your opponent must select a terrain piece within 12” of your battlefield edge. During the battle, friendly fighters may **search** that terrain piece as a scenario action. When searching, roll a die: on a 4+ the item is found and the fighter making the search action picks up a treasure token. 

The contract is completed if that treasure token is in your warband’s control at the end of the battle. 

## 5: High Ground 
For this contract, your opponent must select the tallest terrain piece on the battlefield. During the battle, if one of your fighters climb to the tallest platform of that terrain piece and performs two consecutive wait actions, you have completed the contract. 

## 6: The Target
For this contract, your opponent must select one of your `HEROES` to be **the target**. If that **HERO** remain on the battlefield for the entire battle, you have completed the contract.
