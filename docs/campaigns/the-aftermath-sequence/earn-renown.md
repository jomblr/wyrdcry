# Step 2: Earn Renown
---
In this step, you must roll a dice for each fighter in your warband that took part in the battle and that doesn’t have the `BEAST` or `THRALL` keyword. This is called a **renown roll.** You may add 1 to the roll for each of the following that applies:

- The fighter wasn’t taken out of action during the battle. 
- The fighter took an enemy fighter out of action during the battle

On a 6+, the fighter earns a **level of renown**. In addition, you can select 1 fighter in your warband to be **marked for greatness**. Make 2 renown rolls for that fighter, instead of 1.

- When a `Hero` earns a level of renown, they can either **improve a characteristic** or select a **heroic talent**.
- When a `Henchman` earns a level of renown, they can **improve a characteristic**. However, they cannot improve each characteristic more than once.

:::inverse[The Lad's Got Talent]
When a `Henchman` earns their fourth level of renown, they are promoted to a `Hero`. They immediately lose the `Henchman` keyword and gain the `Hero` keyword instead, and may either improve a characteristic or select a heroic talent.
:::

## Improve Characteristic
---
When improving a characteristic, select one of the fighter's characteristics and increase it by 1. The Health characteristic increases in increments of 2 instead. For Bravery, where a lower value is better, decrease it by 1 instead. A fighter's characteristics can never be improved beyond their racial limit:

| Keyword | Move |Fight |Shoot|Health|Bravery|

## Heroic Talents
---
A `Hero` may select a Heroic Talent from any of the following **specializations**. A fighter cannot select heroic talents from more than two specializations, and can never have more than five heroic talents.
