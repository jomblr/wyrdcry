# Step 2: Earn Renown
---
In this step, you must roll a dice for each fighter in your warband that took part in the battle and that doesn’t have the `BEAST` or `THRALL` keyword. This is called a **renown roll.** You may add 1 to the roll for each of the following that applies:

- The fighter wasn’t taken out of action during the battle. 
- The fighter took an enemy fighter out of action during the battle

On a 6+, the fighter earns a **level of renown**. In addition, you can select 1 fighter in your warband to be **marked for greatness**. Make 2 renown rolls for that fighter, instead of 1.

- When a `Hero` earns a level of renown they must **improve a characteristic**. In addition, on every odd level of renown (1st, 3rd, 5th and so on), they must also select a heroic talent.
- When a `Henchman` earns a level of renown, they must **improve a characteristic**. However, they cannot improve each characteristic more than once.

:::inverse[The Lad's Got Talent]
When a `Henchman` earns their third level of renown, they are promoted to a `Hero`. They immediately lose the `Henchman` keyword and gain the `Hero` keyword instead, and must improve a characteristic and select a heroic talent.
:::

## Improve Characteristic
---
Select one characteristic to improve:

- Add 1 to the fighter's **Move**, **Fight** or **Shoot** characteristics.
- Add 2 to the fighter's **Health** characteristic
- Subtract 1 from the fighter's **Bravery** characteristic

A fighter's characteristics can never be improved beyond their **racial limit** (see the table below).

## Heroic Talents
---
Heroic Talents are traits, reactions and abilities that Heroes can learn once they have earned enough renown. Whenever a `Hero` is instructed to select a heroic talent, they must select from one of the following **talent groups**:

- [Strength](../Talents/1-strength-talents.md)
- [Toughness](../Talents/2-toughness-talents.md)
- [Agility](../Talents/3-agility-talents.md)
- [Perception](../Talents/4-perception-talents.md)
- [Wits](../Talents/5-wits-talents.md)

A fighter can never have heroic talents from more than two different talent groups.

### Legendary Abilities
Each talent group has a ***legendary ability*** that a fighter can select once they have earned 5 levels of renown. Legendary abilities are unique, and no two fighters in the same warband can have the same legendary ability.
