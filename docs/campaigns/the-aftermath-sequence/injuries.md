# Step 1: Roll for Injuries
---

In this step, you must roll a d6 for each fighter that was taken out action during the battle and that doesn't have the `Hero` keyword:

- **On a roll of 1:** the fighter dies from their injuries, or leaves the warband as a cripple.
- **On a 2-6:** the fighter fully recovers.

## Heroes and Injuries
When a `Hero` is taken out of action, they risk suffering a lasting **[injury]** that affects them throughout the campaign. Roll a d66 and apply the corresponding result from the [Lasting Injury Table](#lasting-injuries).

A `Hero` can never have more than three injuries. If they suffer a fourth, they die. If they suffer an **[injury]** they already have, treat that result as a  [Flesh Wound](#flesh-wound) instead.

## Death
Whenever a fighter dies, you must remove them from your warband roster. Roll a D6 for their equipment; on a 3+, it is recovered and added to your warband stash.

:::inverse
### Death of a leader
If your `Leader` dies or is removed from the warband for any other reason, you must select a `Hero` and give them the `Leader` keyword. If you do not have any `Heroes` left, your warband dissolves into infighting and desertion. You must create a new warband to continue the campaign.
:::
