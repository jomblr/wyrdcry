# Step 4: Collect Income
---
In this step, your fighters return to camp and deliver wyrdstone to their faction. This step is broken into 2 sub-steps, which must be completed in order.

## 4.1: Earn favour

Your warband earns 1 favour for each wyrdstone shard delivered to their faction. If they won the battle, they earn 1 additional favour. If your warband earns enough favour to rise in **standing**, they do so.

## 4.2: Collect Income

Collect gold coin according to the warband’s standing

### Income Table
|Favour|Standing|Income|
|---|---|---|
|0–10|Disposable|100gc + 10gc/shard|
|11–25|Recognized|150gc + 15gc/shard|
|26–50|Valued|200gc + 20gc/shard|
|51+|Favoured|250gc + 25gc/shard|
